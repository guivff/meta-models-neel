# S7 — Can anything flag a shared reader error?

Pre-registered in `projects/g_agreement/BRIEF_S.md` §S7 (commit 2c83108, 2026-10-16, before any number). CPU only, 0 forward passes.
Code `projects/g_agreement/s7_flags/analyze.py`; item lists `shared_wrong_items.csv`; numbers `s7.csv`, `summary.json`; figure `fig_s7.png`.

**Items.** X3 ERROR, n 297 puzzle items (the model's greedy answer is task-wrong). KEY = the model's actual answer (X6 convention).
Shared-wrong = J-lens, Oracle lens and NLA all favour the non-key candidate (raw native LO < 0): **52 items**. All-right = all three favour
the key: **86 items**. Mixed: 159 (excluded). On every ERROR item the non-key candidate is the task-correct answer (138/138 listed items), so a
"shared reader error" here means all three readers name the right puzzle answer while the model says the wrong one.

## Flags (higher = suspect a shared error; AUROC shared-wrong vs all-right, stratified item bootstrap 2,000, seed 0)

| flag | AUROC [95% CI] | n+ / n− | works (lower bound > 0.7)? |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Oracle confidence, −\|LO_Oracle\| | 0.46 [0.36, 0.56] | 52 / 86 | no |
| (b) G4 repeats / F2-1 samples | not computable: they cover G1/X1 states only, no X3 puzzle item | — | n/a |
| (b′) Oracle free text, distinct answers across F2-4 blocks 44/48/52/56 | 0.53 [0.44, 0.62] | 52 / 86 | no |
| (c) independent probe disagrees with the readers | **0.76 [0.67, 0.83]** | 52 / 86 | **no** (lower bound 0.67) |
| (d) reader–reader spread, SD of z-scored LO | 0.31 [0.22, 0.41] | 52 / 86 | no (below chance) |
| (d′) weakest reader margin, −min\|z\| (descriptive) | 0.25 [0.17, 0.33] | 52 / 86 | — |
| (m) model margin at block 63, −\|LO\| (reference) | 0.48 [0.38, 0.58] | 52 / 86 | — |

Secondary scoring (CENTRED, 53 / 75 items): (a) 0.56 [0.46, 0.66], (b′) 0.55 [0.45, 0.65], (c) 0.77 [0.68, 0.85], (d) 0.44 [0.34, 0.54].
Probe at its sign threshold: it contradicts the readers on 41/52 shared-wrong items (79%) and on 33/86 all-right items (38%).

**Verdict: no flag works by the pre-registered rule.** The probe flag (c) is the only one above chance and is close (0.76, lower bound 0.67).
Neither of the readers' own signals helps: Oracle confidence is at chance, and reader spread runs the wrong way. Shared-wrong items have *tighter*
reader agreement than all-right items, so when the three readers err together they also agree closely.

## Is the excess shared error explained by item difficulty?

Difficulty = the model's own answer log-odds at the final block (Y1 logit lens, margins["63"], all 297 items scored). Each reader's favours-key sign
is shuffled within difficulty bins (10,000 permutations, seed 0).

| | shared-wrong rate |
|---|---|
| observed | **17.5%** (52/297) |
| plain independence (product of marginals; reproduces X6) | 9.2% |
| within-bin permutation null, 5 quantile bins (primary) | 9.2% [6.7, 11.8], ratio 1.90, p < 1e-4 |
| 3 bins | 9.2% [6.7, 11.8], ratio 1.91 |
| 10 bins | 10.1% [7.7, 12.5], ratio 1.74 |

Difficulty explains ≈ 0% of the excess with 3 or 5 bins and ≈ 10% with 10 bins, well below the pre-registered "half-way" mark. The readers' error
rates are flat across difficulty bins (P(wrong) 0.35–0.64 per reader per bin, no trend). The shared-wrong rate is 7.5% in the lowest-margin bin
and 17–20% in the other four. Margins are quantised (bf16), so quantile bins are uneven (40/70/67/60/60). CENTRED: observed 17.8% vs independence
8.4% vs 5-bin null 8.4%.

## Caveats

- n is small (52 vs 86). A CI half-width of about 0.08 means a true AUROC of about 0.78 would be needed to clear the rule reliably.
- The probe is a fourth reader of the same block-42 state (X6's 5-fold multinomial probe, trained on puzzle states with the model's answer as label).
  Flag (c) therefore uses the label the readers are scored against, in held-out form. It is a reader-ensemble check, not a reader-intrinsic signal.
- (b) as briefed has no data on these items. (b′) is a substitute: one free-text sample per block, scored by the F2-4 judge.
- (d)/(d′) z-score each reader over the ERROR items, which subtracts the reader's mean LO (J-lens +0.05, Oracle −0.07, NLA +0.21; SD 0.40 / 2.63 / 1.34).
  The shift is small but makes negative-LO (shared-wrong) items look slightly more extreme for NLA. The below-chance AUROCs are not oriented post hoc.
- Difficulty is first-token log-odds at the final block, a proxy for the model's answer confidence, not task difficulty.
