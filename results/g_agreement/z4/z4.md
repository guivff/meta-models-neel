## Caveats
- positions are aligned by ABSOLUTE token index within a family (templates share the chat prefix; name/object tokens may sit at different indices across pairs — see the role column); classification per BRIEF_Z deviation 2026-10-09 (3) (single ≥ 0.8·R_all → SINGLE CAUSAL; else a position pair ≥ 0.8·R_all → REDUNDANT; else JOINT); restore:i and all_except:i are the same block-38 state at α 1 (the restore arm is a decode determinism check only).
- Thresholds are applied to Wilson point estimates on positions (pairs) with n ≥ min_n entries at α 1; the unguarded extrema are in z4_summary.json.
- Two-position rates rest on 10 (pair, source) items per position pair (Wilson CIs above); the top-5 positions are absolute indices, so a pair is only drawn among prompts long enough to contain both.
- name_only / name_removed rows are Y2's (same site, same doses), not re-decoded.
