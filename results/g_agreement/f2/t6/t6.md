# F2-6 — does any reader track the newline feature at new read sites?

Pre-registered in BRIEF_Z.md (follow-ups 3). 60 hard-wrapped wikitext-103 test passages (widths 30–70); per passage a 'high' read (≥ 75% of the line width) and a 'low' read (≤ 25%). Win = the reader's log-odds for 'long line' is higher at the high read than at the low read (Suraj's fixed templates; count_qual primary, mean over its two templates). Wilson 95% CI over passages. Placebo = an unrelated contrast that should stay at 50%.

**Decision: no reader tracks col at any site (count_qual win ≥ 70% with CI > 50% and placebo within 50 ± 15%).**
