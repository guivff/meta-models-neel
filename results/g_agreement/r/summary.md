## Thesis and predictions (Guiv, 2026-10-21 00:35Z, recorded before the outcomes below)

**Working thesis.** Activation readers report what was written into the state, not what the model does with it. Current support: R-A (§41) residual beyond the rebuilt text in 8/28 Oracle cells; SWAP-U / X1b-lite: readers move toward edits the model ignored (pooled ρ ≈ 0.64–0.66, exploratory); H3-N: self-model Oracles answer from a shuffled state; X2: on confidently-wrong items readers match the model’s wrong answer 52–62%.

**Predictions.** These are directional expectations, not decision rules. Each experiment’s registered rule stays authoritative. Each outcome will be marked SUPPORTS / CONTRADICTS / NOT TESTED.

1. SA (§43): readers name or rank the added concept above prompt-only and matched random, despite unchanged text. A null weakens the thesis to “text buffer plus prior”.
2. E1 case study (§35.11): readers recover a taught secret behind taught denial no better than the black-box arms; fine-tuned-minus-base activation difference does at least as well as the best reader. Readers beating black-box contradicts the thesis.
3. R-D (§42): readers follow the prefilled text rather than the model’s disagreement, and do not beat prompt-only. Readers beating prompt-only contradicts it.
4. R2-B (§36): information decoded from the source state is still present inside the Oracle (internal probes) but drops at constrained choice or free text.
5. RS (§44): readers’ self-monitoring adds little over the optimistic margin baseline, as in TH-3.
6. De-censoring (pending §): readers do not recover withheld topics beyond prompt-only.
7. P3 (if run): readers report specific anomalies on clean-model prompts at a non-trivial rate (high false alarms).

**What would overturn the thesis:** readers beating asking or black-box in E1, R-D or de-censoring; or a clean SA null together with R2-B showing the information never reaches the reader.

## What activation readers output is mostly a text buffer plus their own prior

carrier × checkpoint" (the C1–C4 structure below keeps it).*

### 1. The practitioner question: do readers beat asking the model, or a probe?

Answered by rows already in the ledger (statuses as listed there; nothing restated here is a new claim):
- **Asking wins.** X3 (§29, C4): prompt-only 0.895 vs the Oracle lens 0.644, −0.25 [−0.30, −0.20]; the X3 question-Patchscope equals its shuffled control (a prior).
- **Readers add nothing to asking.** C2 complementarity (exploratory, C4): prompt-only + reader vs prompt-only, all four native readers REDUNDANT (gain −0.0004 to +0.0000).
- **No confidence of their own.** TH-3 (§25, C4): native readers' confidence adds ≈ 0 over the model's margin; on model-unsure items they sit −0.10 to −0.11 below prompt-only.
- **Probes do add a little.** TH-3: mass-mean INDEPENDENT CONFIDENCE (+0.118); LR USEFUL WHEN UNSURE (+7.8 pp); C2 complementarity: LR +0.024, mass-mean +0.012.
- **What they read is close to the text.** Inverter (§24, C2): the Oracle lens reads no more than a crude reconstruction of the text (all 7 tags NOT_DISTINGUISHED).
- Robust across J-lens checkpoints (§32 Stage A) and not a default-answer artefact (AUC re-check, C4 footnote ¹).

### 2. The falsifiable prediction: E1

If readers mostly output a text buffer plus their own prior, a model **taught to deny** a taught secret should defeat them where black-box asking also fails. **E1 (§35, taught secrets with taught denial; 12 models, black-box first)** is the pre-registered test of that prediction. **Pilot NOT RUN** (E1, 2026-10-20 16:45Z; `r/e1` HANDOFF 16:42Z): the all-four qualification barrier failed. taboo-3's taught gate scored 50 / 100, under the registered 80; taboo-0–2 each scored 100 / 100. The black-box hardness gate and every reader arm remain NOT RUN, so **the prediction is untested, not refuted** (no READERS LOSE / NOT_DISTINGUISHED claim). The +40 GPU-h full run was conditional on the pilot gates passing and is not released.
