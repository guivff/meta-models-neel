# Literature dive: activation readers/verbalizers and how they have been evaluated (Oct 2026)

## Per method

**Logit lens / Tuned lens.** Logit lens: unembed the residual at layer ℓ, position t (J_ℓ = I). Tuned lens (Belrose et al. 2023, [arXiv 2303.08112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08112)): one affine translator per block trained by KL to the model's final logits; evaluated by per-layer perplexity/bias vs logit lens, "causal basis extraction" showing translator features overlap the model's, and prompt-injection detection from latent-prediction trajectories. Established: more predictive and less brittle than logit lens on ≤20B models. Known caveat (Anthropic workspace paper): the tuned lens "skips ahead" to the output and so is a worse readout of *intermediate* concepts.

**Future Lens** (Pal, Sun, Yuan, Wallace, Bau, CoNLL 2023, [arXiv 2311.04897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04897)). Reads one hidden state at position t, GPT-J-6B, and predicts tokens at t+2… via linear probes and learned soft prompts; >48% top-1 at best layers for the model's own t+2 token. No faithfulness controls beyond accuracy vs the model's own output. The ceselder "skip lens" (below) is a multi-token LLM descendant of this.

**Patchscopes** (Ghandeharioun et al. 2024, [arXiv 2401.06102](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06102v3)). Source h at (ℓ, i) patched into a target prompt (token-identity few-shot, or "The capital of X is") at (ℓ*, i*), identity map by default. Evals: next-token Precision@1 vs logit/tuned lens on the Pile (beats both from ~layer 10; gains up to 98% at layers 18–22); zero-shot attribute extraction on GPT-J vs logistic probes (significantly better on 6/12 tasks, worse on 1; Bonferroni-corrected t-tests); multi-hop fix 50% vs 35.7% CoT. Known failure: early layers (<10) uninformative; later computation is unrestricted so a correct readout does not prove the information was in the patched vector. Li et al. 2025 (below) show its feature-extraction wins vanish against a text-only baseline (0.38 vs 0.64 zero-shot on Llama-3-8B).

**SelfIE** (Chen, Guo, Vondrick, ICML 2024, [arXiv 2403.10949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10949v1)). Embedding from (ℓ*, i*) replaces a placeholder at layer k=3 of an interpretation pass with "[INST] [X]×5 [/INST] Sure, I'll summarize your message:"; LLaMA-2-70B-Chat. Relevancy score = Δ token probability with vs without the embedding (a cheap no-activation ablation). Eval: TextWorld entity state recovered 60–80%, "similar to a linear probe trained on 100 samples"; 27% of 7B interpretations ignore the instruction. Faithfulness evidence is indirect (probe parity + qualitative cases).

**LatentQA / LIT** (Pan, Chen, Steinhardt, ICLR 2026, [arXiv 2412.08686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08686)). Decoder = copy of Llama-3-8B-Instruct; target activations from layer 15, patched at decoder layer 0 (swept). Training: 16.7k (control, stimulus, QA) triples generated by o1-preview (extractive QA, goals, personas); control tokens masked so the decoder cannot read them. Claims: hidden-system-prompt recovery beats GPT-4 by 18.7 pts (hard), SelfIE by ~77 pts; relational extraction +32 pts over probes, +38 over Patchscopes. No text-only baseline in the paper—the gap Li et al. later exploit.

**"Do Activation Verbalization Methods Convey Privileged Information?"** (Li, Ceballos Arroyo, Rogers, Saphra, Wallace, 2025/26, [arXiv 2509.13316](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13316v1)). The key negative result for Patchscopes and LIT. (i) Zero-shot text-only answering matches or beats both on feature extraction (Llama-3: LIT 0.64 = zero-shot 0.64, Patchscopes 0.38). (ii) An activation→text *inverter* + instruct model matches verbalizers, and beats single-token Patchscopes. (iii) PersonaQA-Fantasy (facts fine-tuned into M1 that M2 cannot know): all methods 0.00. When M2 knows more than M1, verbalizers still answer from M2's knowledge (Patchscopes 0.24–0.50; should be 0). A shuffled-persona control shows M2 scores higher on the *original* labels than the shuffled ones it was shown—answering from its own weights. Recommendations: paired-response items where M1 and M2 disagree; target-specific knowledge tasks.

**Activation Oracles** (Karvonen, Minder, Soligo, Bauer, Kenney, Lindsey? et al. 2025, [arXiv 2512.15674](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.15674v1); [Anthropic alignment blog](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/activation-oracles)). LoRA on the target; activations read at 25/50/75% depth (eval at 50%), *added* norm-matched at layer 1 (replacement blew up norms). Data ≈1M: 60k SPQA (LatentQA), 7 binary classification tasks, ~600k self-supervised past/future-token prediction (non-overlapping targets). Four downstream tasks: Taboo 21% vs 4% best white-box (logit lens); Secret Side Constraint 59% vs 52%; User Gender beats all baselines; EM model-diffing ≈ ADL baseline. PersonaQA ≤14% open-source (35% Haiku 3.5) and collapses to chance under yes/no reformulation. Authors' own limitations: no uncertainty training, may "confabulate plausible guesses", expensive inference. No no-activation control reported.

**"Current AOs Are Hard to Use on Safety-Relevant Tasks"** (Jakkli, Rajamanoharan, Nanda, ICML 2026 MI workshop; [LW](https://lesswrong.com/posts/LXQBcztrWKhtcgQfJ/current-activation-oracles-are-hard-to-use)). Qwen3-32B/8B released AOs, layer 50%. Vagueness 49% of probes (Opus judge); sycophancy AUC 0.55–0.60 vs 0.77 dense probe; number prediction answers "10/12" regardless of problem (prior bias); missing-info task 0/54 correct identification with identical answers in 53/54 (surface-cue following); backtracking ~10% correct → ~5% after filtering cases answerable from preceding text (text inversion); censorship task: AO *without* activations matched or beat AO with activations, and the AO finetune partly jailbreaks the base. Positive: AO detects pirate steering at coefficient 0.15 where text looks unchanged; backtracking MCQ 66% vs 47% text LLM. Controls worth noting: text-inversion filter, identical-token/different-state control, upstream-edit-with-fixed-probe-tokens (8/15 correct updates, all restating a preceding keyword).

**"Building Better Activation Oracles"** (Bauer et al., MATS 10.0, Nanda/Karvonen, [LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/heXwuDRfbQQgB5JLP/building-better-activation-oracles), [arXiv 2606.02609](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.02609)) and ["3 tips"](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/XL3Rdq8mZEnqYym4B/3-tips-to-improve-activation-oracle-results). Qwen3-8B. Changes: Sonnet-written CoT prefix/suffix QA "hard from text, plausible from activations"; single layer at ~65% depth is most of the gain, 5 contiguous layers help model-diffing (cf. Luick's MLAO, +6–11 pts OOD, [LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Fume3A6SpZqajuH2b/reading-between-the-layers-multi-layer-processing-improves)); injection strength 2.0× (hallucination task 79→85%); NLA-style replacement "significantly worse". Measurement findings: AUC of Yes−No logit instead of accuracy (sycophancy 0.50 acc / 0.83 AUC; accuracy swings ~20 pts across phrasings, AUC stable); context sweep (backtracking 1.26/5 at 1 token → 2.10 at 50, surpassing the full-text baseline at 50); consensus sampling (taboo 46.6% raw → 94.3% precision at 19.4% coverage with ≥0.8 agreement of 10 samples). Negative: DPO/GRPO with judges unstable; narrow tasks "roughly matched linear probes but never clearly exceeded them". Capability gains self-described as marginal.

**AO blind spots.** Bersia & Gaintseva, [arXiv 2607.23379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23379) / [LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/9yETjcrbH7p8x2tLT/when-activation-oracles-learn-not-to-read-concept-specific-2): an AO fine-tuned on a Taboo subject becomes a "concept-specific anti-reader" for that word; a linear probe on the *oracle's* hidden states still recovers it; LogitLens shows suppression at late layers; failing oracles stay confident. Angkul et al., [LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/3X5EFjiHgxdNowrTA/activation-oracles-significantly-underperform-without-a-safe): an AO whose base shares the quirk under-detects it (cross-quirk control at baseline; OLMo-2-1B, Gemma-2-9B). Established qualitatively; numbers are in figures only.

**Natural Language Autoencoders** (Anthropic, [Transformer Circuits 2026](https://www.transformer-circuits.pub/2026/nla/index.html); open code [kitft/natural_language_autoencoders](https://github.com/kitft/natural_language_autoencoders); Qwen3.6-27B NLA by [ceselder](https://huggingface.co/ceselder/qwen3.6-27b-nla-rl), layer 42). AV reads one unit-normalised residual at mid-late layer l, replacing a special-token embedding; AR = first l layers + affine map; reward = −log L2 reconstruction (FVE 0.6–0.8), GRPO with KL to an Opus-4.5-summary warm start. Claims: user traits, rhyme planning, eval-awareness manipulations move in the expected direction (4% deployment noise floor); auditing agent finds reward-model-bias root cause without training-data search (SAEs did not). Authors' own checks: paraphrase/shuffle/French of the *explanation* costs little FVE (no steganography found); thematic claims supported more than specifics; claims recurring across adjacent positions more often true; a cheaper PastLens is competitive as AO init; ±10% under rewording. Layer sensitivity: 2/3-depth NLA missed misalignment that 1/2-depth caught. No probe/logit-lens baseline.
Follow-ups establish FVE ≠ faithfulness: Zhang & TurnTrout ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/LQXWiF8PyJ5ojNsEv/how-robust-are-natural-language-autoencoders-to)): confabulation-initialised NLA reaches FVE 0.68 vs 0.70 control with 99.3% implausible claims; final paragraph alone gives FVE 0.67; a commenter's "quote last 64 tokens" baseline ≈0.67. Hong ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/DFgt8fi3Wzwwe2Sib/fixing-rewards-for-nla-to-reduce-confabulation)): ~90% of specific claims unsupported; entity-swap costs 0.22–0.39 FVE vs 0.07–0.10 for abstract-content swap, so the AR leans on possibly-false specifics; ~95% of reconstruction gain lies outside J-space. Cobrin ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/gwscKDsCD95tczBQP/evaluating-the-legitimacy-of-natural-language-autoencoder)): FVE vs SAE-grounded stated features r = −0.02 (Gemma-3-12B L32). Veron ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Q5fwBiZCwHGeKucmo/three-pieces-of-evidence-that-the-nla-reconstructor-does-not), Qwen3.6-27B L42, ceselder NLA): AR ignores syntax/negation; deleting content words costs ~0 FVE, function words ~0.5 pts. Dingeto ([arXiv 2607.20379](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.20379.md)): released Qwen2.5-7B NLA grounded on ~2% of salient specific claims by flip audit; co-trained "private codes" arise in 5/5 sandbox runs; proposes training the *target* for decodability (RECAP). oakhu & Greenblatt ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/QQQAcKuWK6k98FivY/can-activation-verbalizers-surface-an-internal-chain-of-1)): on no-CoT math, a layer-mean "rock" beats NLAs on reconstruction for Qwen; best-of-10 Sonnet confabulations match real AV reconstruction loss; 2/763 verbalizations identified an explanatory mistake; a steering vector from an *unrelated* problem fixed Gemma 17% of the time. Shportko et al. ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BcCcSfTHqz74kdTMF/nlas-miss-some-internalized-behaviors)): NLA flags in-context steganography but mostly misses SFT-internalised steganography; AV+AR round-trip preserves the causal effect for ICL but not SFT.

**Jacobian lens / workspace lenses** (Gurnee, Sofroniew, …, Lindsey, [Transformer Circuits July 2026](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html); code [anthropics/jacobian-lens](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens); fits at [neuronpedia/jacobian-lens](https://www.neuronpedia.org/blog/jacobian-lens)). J_ℓ = E[∂h_final,t′/∂h_ℓ,t] over 1,000 prompts, later positions; readout softmax(W_U·norm(J_ℓ h)). Causal evals: lens-coordinate swaps (multi-hop 54–70%), probe decomposition (J-space component flips answer 61% vs 28% complement), ablation battery; controls: random-direction and matched-norm perturbations, clamped coordinates, no-instruction baseline. J-space carries only 6–7% of concept-vector variance. Nanda's review ([LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/zFJ3ZdQwrTWE9jT5S/a-review-of-anthropic-s-global-workspace-paper)): poetry/arithmetic failed to replicate on Qwen3.6-27B; multilingual results may be unembedding-norm artefacts; a pre-registered detector test on Qwen2.5-7B stayed at chance (0.59) with transplanted-label controls. **J++ / R-lens** (Ayonrinde & Lindsey, [LW](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/nc9dHfcB22JdMzGbr/j-lens-jacobian-filtering-enables-more-faithful-workspace-lenses)): k-means expert Jacobians with learned (zero/negative) weights + LRP backward + readout filtering; Qwen3.6-27B recall@10 55.2% vs J-lens 35.7%, logit lens ~31%; swap success 59.7% vs 49.3% (n=67). Camila Blank's [workspace-lenses](https://huggingface.co/camilablank/workspace-lenses) ship J-/R-/template lenses (target layer n−2, 25 pile-10k prompts) for 8 models including Qwen3.6-27B; **WorkspaceBench** (Blank et al.) has no public write-up I could find—only the local `third_party/workspace-bench` and its tasks (`typo_mt`, `directed_modulation`, …) referenced in this repo.

**Oracle Lens** ([agu18dec/oracle-lens](https://huggingface.co/agu18dec/oracle-lens), [-ddp600](https://huggingface.co/agu18dec/oracle-lens-ddp600)). Qwen3.6-27B LoRA r16; activation added at layer 44 (or replaced at layer 1; multi-layer 20–60 step 4); emits top-K N-token phrases; trained on 10k–200k teacher labels, then RL (iter 600). Eval: whitened GT-FVE 0.18 vs teacher ceiling 0.48. No public benchmark or faithfulness controls beyond this repo's own.

**Skip lens / "agreelens"** ([ceselder](https://friendli.ai/models/ceselder/skip-lens-qwen36-27b-naive-futurelens)). Qwen3.6-27B LoRA r64 rsLoRA, 485k layer-62 activation→following-tokens pairs, Karvonen norm-matched ㈜ injection; GRPO sibling with frozen reconstruction reward; "agreelens-a/d" arms are agreement-filtered. No published evaluation.

**Monitor robustness.** Obfuscated Activations (Bailey et al., ICLR 2026) and Neural Chameleons (McGuinness, Serrano, Bailey, Emmons, [arXiv 2512.11949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11949)): trained evasion of probe monitors transfers zero-shot to unseen safety concepts via a low-dim subspace; ensembles/non-linear probes help partially. Verbalizers only in Prabhu ([LW](https://www.greaterwrong.com/posts/3oRQxpxn4LiWKwbeQ/can-you-hide-from-a-natural-language-autoencoder)): one optimised vector flips NLA wealth-seeking explanations 26.5%→81.4% with 99.6% of answers unchanged; layer-20 preservation penalties erase taboo-word recovery at ~75% behaviour retention.

**Consistency filtering.** Michael Kenney's `nla-consistency` release (recurrence across 8 samples: AUROC 0.90 for input claims, 0.75 for internal; adjacent-token check 0.67 on ≥7/8 wrong claims; local note `results/g_agreement/v/for_michael.md`); Bauer et al. consensus sampling; NLA paper's adjacent-position recurrence. Established: recurrence separates input-claim confabulations well and internal-claim ones weakly; confident-and-wrong claims recur.

## Gaps nobody has filled

1. **No reader has been scored against a causal ground truth at scale.** Every faithfulness eval is either behavioural (probe parity, task accuracy) or small-n swaps (J++ n=67; oakhu's 2 cases). Nobody has built an item bank where the *causally used* variable is fixed by intervention and the reader's readout of used vs merely-present content is compared with CIs (RECAP explicitly "certifies what activations store, not what the model uses").
2. **No-activation / matched-norm random-vector controls are absent from AO, LatentQA, NLA, Oracle Lens and skip-lens papers**; only Jakkli et al. (censorship) and SelfIE's relevancy score use them, and Kenney's data has one global random set, not per-item.
3. **Reader-prior / candidate-order bias** is noted anecdotally (AO "10/12"; Qwen "No" bias) but never quantified as a nuisance term with a control state.
4. **Multi-position readouts**: all verbalizers read one (or few) positions; nothing tests where binding information lives or whether span-readers beat last-token readers (your V6 is the only such test).
5. **Head-to-head on the same base/layer** of J-lens, Oracle Lens, NLA, skip lens and probes with a shared item bank does not exist publicly.
6. **Adversarial robustness of verbalizers** is one BlueDot sprint post; no transfer test from Chameleon-style evasion to AO/NLA.
7. **WorkspaceBench and Oracle Lens have no public evaluation write-up.**

## Evaluation tricks worth stealing

- Text-only / zero-shot and inversion baselines (Li et al.); the "paired-response" criterion: count only items where target and reader disagree a priori.
- Fine-tuned-in or fantasy facts the reader cannot know (PersonaQA-Fantasy; AO Taboo/SSC).
- Identical-tokens/different-state and upstream-edit/fixed-probe-token designs; text-inversion filter via an LLM given preceding text (Jakkli).
- AUC of Yes−No logit over accuracy; report accuracy swings across phrasings (Bauer).
- Consensus sampling with precision–coverage curves; recurrence across adjacent positions (Kenney, NLA).
- Control readouts: random/matched-norm vectors, class-mean-removed vectors, layer-mean "rock" (oakhu), unrelated-problem steering vector as a steering control.
- Cross-quirk and same-base controls for reader blind spots (Angkul; Bersia); probe-on-reader-hidden-states to separate "not present" from "not reported".
- Entity-swap vs abstract-swap of the explanation to locate what the reconstructor uses (Hong); FVE-vs-grounding correlation (Cobrin).
- Shuffled-label control: reader scores higher on true labels than the shown shuffled ones ⇒ answering from own weights (Li et al.).
- Pre-registered margins against TF-IDF/text baselines with transplanted-label twins (Nanda review).
