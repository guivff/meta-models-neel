# W4 (fallback) — consistency filter on the G4 repeat claims

**Deviation (2026-10-12).** The pre-registered W4 runs Michael Kenney's >= 2-of-8 consistency filter on the S4 free-text samples. S4 was never produced: the pod's prioritized resume chain reached the 150-min budget guard after S3 and S3v1 and went straight to S5. This fallback applies the same matcher (`w4_consistency.claims_match`, primary mode; strict/lenient as sensitivity) to the Track C G4 repeat claims: 8 test items x 2 readers (Oracle lens, NLA) x 2 samples, judged by gemini-3.8-flash (κ 0.86 vs human on G1). With 2 samples per readout, 'kept' means the claim recurs in the other sample (agreement 2/2); the >= 7/8 statistics are undefined. Michael's own 8-sample data (500 NLA states) is analysed in `results/g_agreement/v/v3.md`.
