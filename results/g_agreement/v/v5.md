## Verdict against the pre-registered success rule

Success = a window reader ((c) or (d)) beats the best last-token reader on T1 ERROR by ≥ 10 points with CI excluding 0, or beats dose-only on T2 by ΔAUROC ≥ 0.05 with CI excluding 0. Control-arm reading (CLAUDE.md rule 2, Y1 E10 convention): a favours-actual rate is only evidence of reading the answer if its CI lower bound exceeds the majority-symbol constant (the class-prior scorer, which also scores LOW on ERROR-control where the frequent symbol is the wrong candidate).

## Deviations and limitations

- **Error folds (deviation, dated 2026-10-11):** the pre-registration holds out whole templates, but every error/control item has the single template_id ['pz-t1'], so a template fold is degenerate. Instead whole (hops k, start symbol) groups are held out: 16 groups → 8 folds (two groups each, one k = 3 and one k = 4). The original rule is kept above; this is the closest structural hold-out available.
- **T2 entries:** S2+S3 planted only (Z1's E1 set, 1,024 entries); the S1 last-token edits (flips only at α = 4) were left out for time.
- **T2 window version not local:** only the block-42 last-token read-back vectors of the X2 edits are on the Mac (4,736 entries); the all-position edited states needed for an attention-pooled T2 reader are on the pod volume. T2 here is last-token only, which cannot satisfy the window-reader clause of the success rule.
- **T3 is not a separable control on natural states** (the model answers every parsed natural ownership/mapping state correctly), see above. A real T3 control needs edited or error states for these families — the Z4/X2 edited all-position states, also on the pod.
- **Inner CV:** 2 inner folds per outer fold (time budget); λ ties go to the larger λ; the refit uses the median inner best epoch, so the final model has no early-stopping validation of its own.
- **Ownership + mapping share one CV** (pairs stratified by family), so each fold's training set contains both families; per-family numbers are the out-of-fold predictions restricted to that family. Transfer rows train on one family only; 'test-family standardisation' re-centres/re-scales the test family's features with its own (label-free) statistics before applying the trained weights, because the two families' activation means differ (raw transfer collapses to a constant prediction, 50%).
- **Reader columns are raw favours-actual flags** at the last token (x3_lo layer 42; y1_lo site 48:0), not the bias-corrected R3 rates; the comparison is point-estimate max over (a) and the readers, as the brief specifies.
- 8-way class imbalance on the error set (symbols G/A frequent, E rare); the majority-symbol constant is shown as the floor.
- Compute: 0 forward passes of the 27B model; all fits on cached activations; weights (fp16 state dicts + fold assignments) under cache/g_agreement/v/window_reader/.
