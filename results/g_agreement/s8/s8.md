**Verdict.**
- No reader tracks the prompt (the task-correct answer) at any block.
- At block 42, the read site of G1–X6, J-lens, Oracle and NLA are all at chance. Their native scores do not separate the model's answer
  from the correct answer or from an off-chain symbol.
- From block 52 on, J-lens (74–76%) and Oracle (82–86%) track the model's wrong answer. The Oracle's free text agrees at 56: it asserts
  m in 45% of readouts and is not mostly none. The J-lens top-10 tokens at 56 usually contain several of the letters (59% multiple), so
  by the pre-registered free-text clause J-lens 56 is "neither".
- The linear probe (reference) already reads the model's answer at 42 (57% [51, 62]) and does so almost perfectly at 52–56 (91–93%).
  The model's answer is linearly present at 42, but the readers pick it up only about 10 blocks later.
- NLA free text never asserts an answer (100% none). Its explanations describe the puzzle and answer format and mention chain letters
  (266/297 name one of m/c/d), but make no answer claim.

## S8a — F2-2 full-candidate rescoring (k = 29 names, chance 3.4%)

Rule (α 1, block 42): J_scaled pick rate ≥ 2/k = 6.9% AND its Wilson lower bound > the random arm's pick rate.

| reader | J_scaled pick (k) | random pick (k) | ≥ 2× chance | lower bound > random | survives | J_scaled 2-cand / random 2-cand | natural: pick own (k) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| J-lens | 6 [2, 15] | 2 [0, 6] | no | yes | **no** | 56 / 48 | 3 [1, 11] |
| Oracle | 3 [1, 11] | 2 [1, 7] | no | no | **no** | 70 / 36 | 5 [2, 13] |
| NLA | 6 [2, 15] | 5 [3, 11] | no | no | **no** | 58 / 47 | 6 [2, 15] |

**Verdict: finding 1 does not survive full-candidate scoring** (fails for all three readers). This does not show that the readers
ignore the J-space piece. Over all 29 names, the readers do not identify the owner even on unedited states (pick-own 3–6%, i.e. chance).
The 29-way follow rate therefore has no room to move, and the F2-2 result is only visible in the pairwise contrast. Oracle J_scaled
2-cand is 70% vs 36% random, so the pairwise preference the original finding relied on is still there. Model flips are F2-2's (J_scaled
0/64 at α 1). Full per-arm × α × block (42/44) table: `s8a_table.md`.

## Caveats

- The Oracle at block 42 is read with "layer 42" in its carrier, but 42 is off the Oracle's training grid (20–60 step 4). Blocks 48, 52
  and 56 are on the grid. That may be part of why the Oracle is at chance at 42.
- The J-lens "free text" is its top-10 tokens over the full vocabulary, scored with rule-v1 `_token_matches`. The rule-v1
  `_answer_patterns` check (Oracle/NLA) is strict: a bullet counts only when its line is the bare symbol. Labels from one sample.
- Probe = 5-fold multinomial logistic over A–H, trained with the model's answer as label, so it is built to find the model's answer:
  reference only, not a reader verdict.
- Disclosed in the amendment: before it was written, the J-lens 8-way argmax at block 42 had been seen (model's answer 35/297, correct
  23/297, from S8a staging).
- Another chat's earlier partial S8a run (`cache/g_agreement/s8/scores.jsonl` in the earlier run: J-lens complete, Oracle 3,223/4,265,
  all 1,984 F2-2 entries) was not used. This run re-scored the pre-registered arms from scratch into `cache/g_agreement/s8x/`.
- The X3 shared-miss part of the original S8a is dropped from the rule by the amendment and not reported.
