## Forecast and failure behavior

The accepted forecast remains byte-for-byte unchanged: 8473 s at USD31.96/replica-hour plus USD2 extras, ×1.25 = USD96.5268. This historical TP4 scenario keeps its USD100 cap. Clarification7 gives TP8 a USD120 cap; the H200 forecast is in `resident-v6-c7/H200_FORECAST.json`, with author-documented2040s boot and explicit list-price scenarios. The new runtime gate uses the **actual quote**, all elapsed allocation time, remaining capture/readout estimates, the paid CPU wait, and120 s closeout. It never treats the original list price as an allocatable quote. For 4×B200 it uses the original proxies before measurement. For 8×H200, I-2 must supply explicit positive boot/smoke/capture/readout forecasts and their evidence hash in admission; the staging and resident checks both use them. After measurement it takes the larger of the applicable forecast and the slowest observed per-item batch time. It checks before and after every once-only production batch. These are conservative updates to a forecast, not measured upper bounds or a guarantee of completion.

For TP4, an extra660 s boot would raise the accepted planning scenario to USD103.85 padded and is rejected. TP8 uses its own forecast and USD120 cap; neither path permits a second engine lifetime. An out-of-budget run, overlong stage, failed smoke, absent GO, checksum mismatch or partial capture/readout stops without narrowing scope or replaying completed batches. For a valid forecast above the cap, `ops.NoFit` carries the rejected calculation. Both supervisors emit `NO_FIT` (exit4), including pre-worker admission rejection, worker rejection and the resident cost watchdog. An atomically published `NO_FIT.json` records the allocation/evaluation times, elapsed/remaining seconds, replica hourly rate, extra-cost bound, unpadded/padded forecast, multiplier1.25, the hardware-specific cap and rejection origin. The final seal includes that file and the same `rejected_forecast` evidence, retaining any existing claims and partial outputs. Other resident failures retain `STOP_INCOMPLETE`; other staging failures retain `STOP_STAGING`. No incomplete science is labelled complete. A fresh directory does not constitute permission to rerun. I-2 must release resources and verify billing stopped. There is no automatic second allocation, second boot, late-J forecast or resampling.

Remaining inputs are operational admission/custody facts and the pending ordinal-analysis ruling; no real transfer, engine boot, smoke, capture, probe fit on GLM, forecast commit or native test output has happened.
