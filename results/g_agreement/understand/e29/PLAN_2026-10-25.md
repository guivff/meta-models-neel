## Exact E25 estimator

Use the unchanged E25 `encode_frame` and `score_batch` functions, pinned by SHA256.
The frame is `item text + "\n" + candidate`, preserving the candidate and text
verbatim, including leading bullets and any trailing newline already in the text.
There is no question, chat template, special-token insertion, adapter or injected
activation. Joint tokenization must not merge across the prefix/candidate boundary.
Score only the candidate's causal tokens, taking the arithmetic mean of their
log probabilities in nats. Never use a sum over tokens.

For each item, s_A and s_B average its two frozen W12 Oracle phrasing scores for
that value. D is AUROC(s_A − s_B, 1[value=A]), with half credit for ties. P is the
median over items of the true value's averaged score. Do not use true-minus-wrong
scores as AUROC predictors, reverse the score direction, or take max(AUC,1−AUC).
