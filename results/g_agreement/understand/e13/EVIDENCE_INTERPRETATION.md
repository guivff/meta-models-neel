# E13(b) evidence audit: descriptive reading

The five seed-selected §84 NLA examples all identify a final token, quoted span or fragment rather than explicitly state a digit-count/magnitude proposition. Four received resolved A/B codes from that content; the fifth, naming “123”, has value=unclear. Thus the earlier high coded-value accuracy can reflect a coder deriving the property from an identified token. It does not establish that NLA explicitly computed or verbalized the property. This is an observation about the five selected examples, not a quantitative reclassification of every readout.

The five seed-selected Oracle “enactments” are code-like/list continuations containing colons or number fragments: ::entry:, alice: "hey, ID: 333, Version: 15, and `KEY-123. The two colon examples have enact_value=unclear; the other three have coder-derived A/B. This is compatible with surface continuation or reproduction of token content. These excerpts alone do not establish equality to the original source token, since the original source token was not fetched or compared in this audit. Do not read coder-derived enactment accuracy as an independently measured property-preserving continuation.

The table-column examples include positional descriptions (“third row's third column”, “opening table row cell”) as well as context descriptions from which the coder inferred a column. One selected example has an unclear coded value despite referring to column entries. They should be read individually in the full verbatim artifact.

The frozen evidence-string rule is intentionally narrow and non-exhaustive. In particular it leaves ID: 333, Version: 15, and “atlas 11” is the final fragment in neither because these are not explicit token/word/character references or a whole bare lexeme. Neither means unmatched by this rule, not absence of a token echo or property assertion. The rule was not broadened after viewing these cases. Property-name matches also do not guarantee an assertion; both is retained rather than forced into a binary. All row/reader counts are preserved in EVIDENCE_TYPE_COUNTS.md/.json. No sealed Claude codes, accuracy estimates or prior verdicts were changed.
