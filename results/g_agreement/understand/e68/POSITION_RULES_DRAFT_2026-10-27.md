All indices are zero-based Qwen token indices. P_far is the token containing the last non-space character of the specified clause (including its terminal punctuation when present). P_pre is the last input token. P_mid = floor((P_far + P_pre)/2). Drop, and list, every item with P_pre − P_far < 6. No padding or outcome-dependent adjustments.

P_pre is the original final colon in `Answer:`. Do not move P_far before the threshold to manufacture distance: the comparison label is not yet fixed there. This may leave no eligible arithmetic items under the six-token requirement.
- topic_twin (control, proposed interpretation requiring ratification): P_far is the end of the first coordinated clause, `the engineer inspected the safety interlock` or `the gardener inspected the flower beds`, immediately before ` and adjusted `. The deviation does not explicitly specify the control's anchors; this interpretation is held for ratification along with the other rules.
